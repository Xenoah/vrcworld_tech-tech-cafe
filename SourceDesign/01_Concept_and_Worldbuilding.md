# Concept and world setting

## Name
**THE COMMONS - Compact Edition**

## Core concept
A "third place" for the metaverse: not merely a bar, not merely a conference room, and not merely a club. It is a small civic salon where ideas are presented publicly, challenged over drinks, continued in quiet rooms, and eventually dissolved into music.

The world should feel *occupied before it feels impressive*. Every major feature is visible from the central atrium so a newcomer understands the social temperature in seconds.

## Fiction / setting
THE COMMONS occupies a renovated technical exchange building in a dense future city. The building was once a data relay and public observatory. Its old central media core remains as a suspended holographic globe and presentation wall. Independent researchers, creators, DJs and small communities gradually converted the surrounding galleries into a cafe, bar and night salon.

The place has no exclusive "members only" mythology. The fiction should communicate that anyone may arrive curious and leave knowing someone.

## Emotional sequence
1. **Arrival** - readable, safe, no immediate social collision.
2. **Orientation** - see stage, bar, upper cafe and city at once.
3. **Low-risk entry** - cafe edge, posters, or bar stools.
4. **Participation** - main lounge, Q&A, stage, DJ floor.
5. **Deepening** - upper cafe / quiet room / terrace.
6. **Late-night decompression** - lighting warms, visual motion slows, conversations become primary.

## Signature motifs
- Circular rings around the central media core, representing orbiting conversations.
- Warm timber and amber light at human scale, dark metal at architectural scale.
- Blue/cyan light only on information, media and music systems.
- Dense greenery at transitions rather than everywhere.
- City view used as visual breathing space.
- Text graphics should be sparse, short and legible, not wallpaper typography.
